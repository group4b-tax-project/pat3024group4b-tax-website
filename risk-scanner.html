AaDab Integrity & Section 140 Risk Scanner

This module provides an interactive educational tool to evaluate the ethical and compliance risks of corporate tax planning strategies.

The assessment is based on the principles of Section 140(1) of the Income Tax Act 1967, which gives the Director General of Inland Revenue powers to address certain arrangements involving tax avoidance.

Understanding Section 140(1) ITA 1967

Section 140(1) provides powers to the Director General of Inland Revenue to disregard or vary certain transactions or arrangements where the provision applies to tax avoidance or tax evasion.

Companies should ensure that their tax planning arrangements have genuine commercial purposes, proper documentation and comply with the relevant tax laws.

Important Learning Note

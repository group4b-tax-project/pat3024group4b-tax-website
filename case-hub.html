Tax Relevance

The company incurred annual motor vehicle lease rentals of RM60,000. The lease commenced on 1 November 2024 for a period of four years. The price of the car when new was RM190,000.

These details are relevant when examining the deductibility of motor vehicle lease rentals under Section 39(1)(k) of the Income Tax Act 1967.
